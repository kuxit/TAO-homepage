01About Me

Hello! I am Tao Xie (谢涛), a fourth-year B.S. student in the Outstanding Engineer Class of Automation at Guangdong University of Technology, advised by Prof. Yiqun Zhang. I conduct research in the SIGMTA group at the OMG Lab and work closely with Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung.

My current research interests center on sequence modeling and feature interaction. I study how to model both within a unified framework. Most of my work to date has focused on time series, though my interests extend to other forms of sequential data. My work covers supervised classification, anomaly detection, forecasting, representation learning, and unsupervised pattern discovery and clustering, with applications in sepsis classification and industrial detection. Related work has appeared at KDD, BIBM, and PRICAI. I have also led or served as a core member of several national- and provincial-level undergraduate research and innovation projects.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing basketball and listening to music.
