Bio

Hello! I am Tao Xie (谢涛), an incoming direct-entry Ph.D. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I will join the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS) in Fall 2027 under the supervision of Prof. Zheng Lin.

I am currently a final-year undergraduate in the Outstanding Engineer Class of Automation at Guangdong University of Technology, advised by Prof. Yiqun Zhang at the OMG Lab. I also work closely with Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung through research collaboration and academic exchange.

Generally, I work in AI, learning to build as an engineer and think with taste as a researcher.

My previous research focused on interpretable sequence modeling and feature interaction, primarily in time-series settings. I studied temporal dependencies and cross-variable interactions for applications including early risk prediction, anomaly detection, and decision support.

My current research interests focus on large language models (LLMs) and natural language processing (NLP).

Beyond research and work, I cherish individual independence and sincere exchanges of thought, journeying through life’s trials in a lifelong quest for love and freedom.
